What is the fundamental difference between negative reinforcement and punishment?
Tap card to reveal answer Space
Answer & Grounding
Negative reinforcement removes an unpleasant stimulus to increase behavior, while punishment introduces an unpleasant consequence to decrease it.
Key point: Reinforcement always strengthens behavior; punishment always reduces it.
